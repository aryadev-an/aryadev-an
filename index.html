From waste stream to usable resource

Every site produces a different mix. The infrastructure follows the same four stages.

1

Collect

Wet, dry, sewage and effluent are separated at source before they mix.

2

Treat

STP and ETP for water, biogas for organics, incinerators for what remains.

3

Recover

Water, gas, compost and scrap come out as material you can use or sell.

4

Return

Reused water and energy go back to the site. Residue is disposed of safely.
